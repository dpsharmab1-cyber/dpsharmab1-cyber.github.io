Summary

  • Without an account, your projects stay in your browser. We never receive them.
  • With an account, we store your email, your saved projects and your payment records, to run the Service for you.
  • Card, UPI and bank details go to Razorpay, not to us.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time: .

1. Who we are

("we") runs Pack Studio and is responsible for the personal data described here. We aim to handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and their rules.

2. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address (and your name and profile picture, if you sign in with Google)When you create an accountTo sign you in and contact you about your account

We do not collect card numbers, UPI IDs or bank details. Razorpay collects these directly under its own privacy policy.

3. Data stored in your browser

If you save projects without signing in, they are kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on your device and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them. When you sign in, your browser also keeps a sign-in token so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

4. How we use data

Only to provide and improve the Service: signing you in, saving and syncing projects, processing payments and refunds, providing support, keeping the Service secure, and meeting legal obligations such as tax record-keeping. We will ask for your consent before using your data for anything else, such as marketing emails, and you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run the Service, and only as much as they need:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your account and saved projects are stored with Supabase.
  • Razorpay: processes payments.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Our email provider: sends sign-in links and account emails.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may log basic technical data.

Some providers may process data outside India. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Saved projects are kept until you delete them or close your account. Account data is deleted within 30 days of a deletion request, except payment records, which we keep for as long as tax and accounting laws require. Server logs are kept for a short time for security.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to: tell you what personal data we hold about you; correct or update it; delete it; or withdraw consent you have given. You can delete individual projects yourself in the Service. To make any other request, email from the address on your account. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules make sure each account can read only its own projects and payments, and payment confirmations are cryptographically verified. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

The Service is meant for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 without a parent's or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes
