FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are the dielines accurate enough to print?

They are drawn at true 1:1 scale with cut, crease, bleed and glue marks, and account for board thickness. We are still test-printing every template with partner printers, so ask your printer to check the file before a large run.

Which file formats can I download?

SVG and PDF dielines (PDF uses CutContour and Crease spot colours), DXF for CAD and cutting tables, GLB 3D models, PNG mockup renders, and an SVG proof with your artwork.

Do I need to install anything or sign up?

No. It runs in any modern browser on desktop or phone, and you can save projects in your browser without an account. Sign in with email or Google to keep them on every device.

Can I use my own artwork?

Yes. Add text, logos, images, shapes and pack labels (veg mark, MRP and legal details, nutrition table, EAN-13 barcode, QR code) on any panel with the artwork editor, or download the dieline, design it in your own tools at 1:1 and upload the full artwork back.

What is coming next?

Rigid boxes, displays and cups, saved projects, AI design suggestions, and ordering prints from partner printers.
